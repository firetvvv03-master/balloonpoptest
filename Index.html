📖 Codex & In-Depth Game Guide

🎮 How to Play

Tap or click any active balloon on screen to throw your piercing needle/projectile at it. Each balloon has an HP pool. Pop them before they float off the top of the screen to avoid taking HP damage. Collect coins, and buy upgrades or pets to conquer endless waves!

⚡ Weather Cycles & Thunderstorm

Dynamic weather changes every 30 seconds! During the **Thunderstorm & Electric Surge** cycle, lightning flashes across the arena, zapping random balloons for heavy damage and boosting your score & coin gain with an Electric Surge!
